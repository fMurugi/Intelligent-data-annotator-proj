About the Project

This project aims to provide a comprehensive suite of geospatial analysis tools that are accessible to anyone interested in studying environmental changes. The tools include vegetation cover analysis, deforestation reporting, and trends related to climate change impacts on ecosystems.

Geospatial Environmental Tools

This map visualization showcases key environmental insights and trends, including deforestation, vegetation cover, and NDVI trends, to monitor climate change and its impact on ecosystems.
